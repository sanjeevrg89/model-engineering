Beyond the chapters

GPU capstones

Four probes that test the course's predictions on real hardware: weigh a checkpoint, fill a context, count active experts, diffuse a batch. Scripts, a recorder and a self-test live in capstones/.

The course has no GPU, so its numbers are floors and config arithmetic. The capstones are where those predictions meet a real card. Each probe records its machine, versions and command with the result, and the recorder refuses to publish a run without them.

1The four probes

  1. Weigh it. Load each roster checkpoint's configs and shard headers, sum resident bytes by dtype, and compare against the course's weight tables.
  2. Fill it. Grow one sequence to the model's max context on the reference SKU and record where allocation fails, against chapters 20 and 23.
  3. Route it. Count distinct experts touched over a fixed workload for each MoE roster model, against chapters 11, 12 and 15.
  4. Denoise it. Time LLaDA-8B diffusion steps at several mask ratios on one GPU, against chapter 25's quality-speed knob.

Probes ship as the roster grows: probe 1 with the first edition, probes 2–4 as their chapters land. Until then this page is the plan, and the plan is honest about that.
