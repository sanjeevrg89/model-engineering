The attention zoo

Every attention variant that matters since 2022, one line each: what it does, whether this course pins it, cites it or scopes it out, and where to read more. Share this page; argue with it via the issue form.

Status key: pinned means a roster config or the pinned Transformers tree demonstrates it and the course quotes the lines. Cited means the course states the paper's or release's claims with attribution and never its config values. Out of scope means pre-2022 classics or other tracks, named so the map has no silent gaps. Paper numbers below are the papers' own reported figures.
